Exploratory coalesced-encoder attempt (rejected)
------------------------------------------------
After the frozen two-process negative, an experimental backend merged reset,
pack, evaluator, and transition into one encoder per epoch. Explicit resource
barriers covered packed counts after reset; packed frames/ranks/count after
pack; and evaluator output after evaluate. Epoch encoder boundaries retained
full transition-to-next-pack ordering. The original multi-encoder path remained
available via SOMA_METAL_DYNAMIC_MULTI_ENCODER. Backend SHA-256 for this
experiment: 8d09859558afd34989a5794ad13dd12278234da5cabd179cf97ca9c121c1e658. Dynamic grouped/generic, unknown-class, width-1/32,
and fallback tests passed exactly. One full 3x11 reuse process also remained
exact, but it was slower in absolute terms and grouped still lost: batch raw
median G/S values were 1.132, 1.086, and 1.076. Resource-scoped barrier overhead
made the change counterproductive, so it was reverted rather than shipped.
