Full-run medians (descriptive only; non-isolated n=3): Metal physical-backend
call is 10.4x faster than CPU collective (0.128s vs 1.327s), but it is 3.2x
slower than the independent host sensing loop (0.128s vs 0.040s). Full Metal
wall is 1.23x host-reference wall (34.742s vs 28.274s), not a speedup. Its
remaining host work is 99.6% of wall time. Metal wall is 0.95x CPU-collective
wall in this sample, but the raw ranges overlap substantially; no full-wall
backend ordering is claimed from three non-isolated samples.
